The craft

Trained hands, honest tools

Every barber here served an apprenticeship before they touched a paying head of hair. We still strop our razors on leather, not because it's quaint, but because it's the sharpest edge you can get.

We keep the shop small on purpose. Five chairs means every barber knows your name, your cowlick, and what you actually meant last time you said “just a little off the top.”

Around the shop

A working shop, not a showroom. Leather chairs, straight razors, and a record player that never stops.
